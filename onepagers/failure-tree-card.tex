\cardtitle{When it breaks}{Budgets are decided now. When one runs out, take the fallback.}

\warn{``I am nearly there''} is true about half the time. You cannot tell which
from the inside. That is why the budget is set in advance.

\vspace{3pt}

\section{Every fallback has three parts}
What is not working \dash\ what I am doing instead \dash\ \warn{what that
costs}. The third is the one people leave out and the one that separates a
decision from a shortcut.

\section{Do not}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Debug in silence. Thirty seconds is fine; two minutes is dead time.
        Narrate: what I think is wrong, what I am checking, what would confirm it.
  \item Restart from scratch. It feels clean and it is never right.
  \item \warn{Disable the check.} The one action that changes their assessment
        of your judgement rather than your luck.
  \item Apologise more than once.
\end{itemize}

\section{Recovering}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item Say where you are against the plan, once, aloud.
  \item Re-cut to the \textbf{next checkpoint only} \dash\ not the whole session.
  \item \warn{Protect the demonstration block.} Last to be given up, not first.
\end{enumerate}
