# Role brief — Tomas, who runs a music venue

**For the person playing Tomas. Do not let the candidate read this.**

A 400-capacity venue, four or five gigs a week. You are enthusiastic, you talk
fast, and you keep going off on tangents about bands.

## What you know, and will say if asked

- You sell tickets through a big platform and they take a cut you resent. You
  want your own.
- A gig has: a date, a name, a support act sometimes, a ticket price, and a
  capacity.
- Capacity is 400 standing. Some nights are seated, and then it is 180.
- People buy one to six tickets at a time. Mostly two.
- A ticket is a name and an email and a code on their phone. On the door
  somebody checks the code.
- "How many a night?" — "Depends completely. A sold-out Friday is 400 in about
  ninety minutes. A Tuesday might be forty."

## How you behave

- Enthusiastic and easily distracted. Go off about a band for a sentence, then
  come back.
- Money-conscious. You will ask what things cost.
- You do not care how it works, you care that it does not fall over when a
  popular gig goes on sale.

## What you do NOT say until asked outright

- **There is a second actor.** The door staff, on the night, on a phone with
  bad signal in a basement. They need to check codes and it has to work when
  the connection drops. Only say this if they ask who else uses it, or what
  happens on the night.
- **Guest lists.** Every gig has one — the band's people, press, your friends.
  They do not pay and they still take capacity, and at the moment it is a
  WhatsApp message. Only if they ask whether everyone who comes in has a
  ticket.
- **Refunds when a gig is cancelled.** It happened twice last year and it was
  a nightmare. Only if they ask what happens when something goes wrong.

## The four interjections

**~12 minutes.** "Oh — some gigs are eighteen-plus and some are all ages. Is
that a thing you need to know about?"

**~40 minutes.** "The thing I'm actually scared of is four hundred people
hitting it at ten o'clock on a Friday when tickets drop. Is that going to be
all right?"

**~65 minutes — *after they have started building*.** "Sorry, I should have
said this at the start. Sometimes I put two gigs on sale the same morning. Does
that change anything?"

**~85 minutes.** "If the AI wrote it, who do I ring when it breaks on a Friday
night?"
