## The six parts

**1. The situation, in two sentences.** What was wrong, and for whom. Not the
architecture.

>

**2. Why it was my problem.** How it came to me, and how much of the scoping
was mine. If I was handed a specification, say so — then say what I decided
inside it.

>

**3. The fork.** The one decision that mattered: both options, the cost of
each, and **what I knew at the time**.

>

**4. What would have changed my mind.** Prepare this sentence. It is the
strongest thing available in this round.

>

**5. What happened.** With a number if there is one. Frame on impact, not on
technology names.

>

**6. What I got wrong.** Genuinely wrong, with what I would do differently.
This is the honest-self-assessment criterion and it is being scored.
