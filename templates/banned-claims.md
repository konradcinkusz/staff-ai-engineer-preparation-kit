## The list

| Sentence I must not say | Why my evidence does not support it | What I say instead |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

Common shapes, to check yourself against:

- [ ] A capability my integration has never exercised against the live thing
- [ ] A count I cannot reproduce on the day — tests, scenarios, improvement
- [ ] An outcome I contributed to, stated as though I owned it
- [ ] A superlative — *only*, *first*, *every time*, *always*. The most
      dangerous entries, because one counterexample is enough and they often
      have one
- [ ] Work by somebody else that I was near

## The one that would cost most

If I say one thing on this list, which one ends the conversation?
